# Model Card: Arabic Pharma-Match (Fine-tuned Jina-v3)

## Model Details

* **Developer:** Mohammed Mohsen (AI Engineer)
* **Model Type:** Sentence Transformer / Embedding Model
* **Base Model:** `jina-embeddings-v3`
* **Language(s):** Arabic, English (Mixed pharmaceutical nomenclature)
* **Training Framework:** `sentence-transformers`, `PyTorch`
* **Status:** Trained & Validated (Final Model Saved)

## Intended Use

* **Primary Task:** Semantic Text Matching for pharmaceutical products.
* **Target Application:** Reconciling supplier medicine lists with a standardized system master list for the "Cluster" ERP ecosystem.
* **Out-of-Scope:** Clinical diagnosis or prescription recommendations.

## Training Methodology

The model was fine-tuned using a **Triplet Loss** objective. It maximizes cosine similarity between standard medicine names (Anchors) and supplier variations (Positives), while pushing away visually similar but pharmacologically different products (Hard Negatives).

### Training Pipeline:

1. **Dataset:** 45,724 training samples derived from 37,597 medicines and their supplier variations.
2. **Hard Negative Mining:** Integrated `rapidfuzz` to ensure the model distinguishes between highly similar text strings.
3. **Cross-Validation:** 5-Fold K-Fold strategy used to ensure stability across the dataset.

## Training Results & Metrics

The model demonstrated consistent improvement and high precision across all folds.

### Final Performance Metrics:

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Best Validation Accuracy (Cosine)** | **99.31%** (Fold 0, Epoch 6) |
| **Average Training Loss (Final Phase)** | **~0.095** |
| **Total Training Epochs** | 7 |
| **Samples Processed** | 45,724 |

### Epoch Performance Summary:

* **Initial Epoch:** Started strong with **98.79%** accuracy.
* **Peak Performance:** Reached **99.31%** in Epoch 6.
* **Convergence:** Final training on the full dataset achieved a low training loss of **0.0973**, suggesting the model has generalized well without significant overfitting.

## Factors & Limitations

* **Domain Sensitivity:** Highly optimized for Egyptian pharmaceutical nomenclature.
* **Robustness:** Includes data augmentation (word dropout/char noise), making it resistant to common data entry typos found in supplier invoices.
* **Execution:** Final model weights are stored in `./saved_models/final_model`.

## Evaluation

The success metric used was `cosine_accuracy` within a `TripletEvaluator`, which confirms that in 99.31% of cases, the model correctly placed the matching product closer in vector space than the incorrect "hard negative" product.
